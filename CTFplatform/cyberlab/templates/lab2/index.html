Category: SQL Injection + Account Enumeration

Objective

Log in to /lab2/login as the team's admin account — without knowing its password, and without being told its username. Nothing on the login page reveals it this time.

{% if mode != 'challenge' %}
Why this happens in the real world

Two separate bugs chain together here. First: a "forgot password" feature that responds differently depending on whether a username exists lets you enumerate valid accounts before you've broken anything. Second: the login form still builds a raw SQL string from your input — a stray quote can end that string early and let you add your own logic.

Where to start
  • Look for any account-related feature besides the login form itself. Something that reacts differently to "this account exists" vs. "it doesn't" is a classic real-world leak.
  • The team roster isn't secret (sumit, ayush, tejas, sahil, sayali) — but none of those are the admin account. Think about what an ops/admin account is usually named, and confirm your guess with the tool from the previous hint.
  • Once you have a real username, the injection itself still works the classic way — but the login endpoint now strips the most common comment sequence out of your input. That doesn't mean commenting out the rest of the query is impossible; SQLite understands more than one way to do it.
